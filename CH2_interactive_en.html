Core Concepts at a Glance: Classifying Annuities

Accurately distinguishing between annuity types is key to correct calculation. The timing of cash flows determines the difference in value.

Core Concept: Cash flows occur at the end of each period.

Real-World Example: Monthly mortgage or car loan payments, bond coupon payments.

Present Value Formula: $PVA=C[\frac{1-\frac{1}{(1+r)^{n}}}{r}]$

Core Concept: Cash flows occur at the start of each period. Since the money arrives one period earlier, its value is $(1+r)$ times that of an ordinary annuity.

Real-World Example: Rent payments, a regular savings plan that starts at the beginning of each month.

Present Value Formula: $PVAD = PVA \times (1+r)$

Core Concept: An equal cash flow that continues indefinitely; this is the basis of the zero-growth dividend model.

Formula: $PVP=\frac{C}{r}$
