Core Concept Overview: Bonds and Equity

A quick review of bond cash flow structure and the key assumptions of the valuation models.

Bond Cash Flow Structure:

  • Coupon: Interest received periodically, treated as an annuity.
  • Face Value (FV): The principal repaid at maturity, treated as a single cash flow.

Relationship Between Price and Interest Rate:

  • Premium: Coupon rate > market interest rate ($r$) $\Rightarrow$ Price > Face Value
  • Discount: Coupon rate < market interest rate ($r$) $\Rightarrow$ Price < Face Value
  • Par: Coupon rate = market interest rate ($r$) $\Rightarrow$ Price = Face Value

GGM is a simplified equity valuation model whose main assumptions include:

  • Perpetual Dividend Growth: Dividends grow perpetually at a constant rate ($g$).
  • $r > g$ Restriction: The investor's required rate of return ($r$) must be strictly greater than the dividend growth rate ($g$), otherwise the result is meaningless.
  • Applicable To: Suitable for mature, stably growing companies that pay dividends.
